A **fever** is a temporary increase in body temperature, usually as a response to an illness or infection. It is a common sign that the body is fighting off something, such as a virus, bacteria, or other foreign substances.### Key Points About a Fever:- **Normal body temperature** is typically between **97°F (36.1°C)** and **99°F (37.2°C)**.- A **fever** is generally considered to be a body temperature of **100.4°F (38°C)** or higher.- Fevers are usually **not harmful** in themselves, but they can be a sign that something is wrong in the body.### Common Causes of Fever:- **Infections** (viral, bacterial, fungal, or parasitic)- **Inflammation** (such as from autoimmune diseases)- **Cancer** (in some cases)- **Vaccinations**- **Heat exhaustion
